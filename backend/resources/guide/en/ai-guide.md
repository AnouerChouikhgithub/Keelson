The assistant answers questions about how Keelson works. It reads two things: the product documentation and your role in the organization. It never reads your workspace, so it cannot see or repeat a customer, an invoice, a member or an audit row, and it will not pretend otherwise.

Questions are 3 to 500 characters, in English, French or Arabic, and the answer comes in the language you ask in. Each answer names up to three documentation articles it was built from.

Ask about organizations, roles and invitations, customers, invoice states, notifications, the audit trail or AI settings. If the documentation does not cover the question, the assistant says so without contacting a model, so an uncovered question costs nothing.

The answer is plain text: no links, no images and no markup. Treat it as guidance and confirm anything consequential on the screen itself.
