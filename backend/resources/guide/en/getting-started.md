Create an account with an email address, a display name, an organization name and a password of 10 to 72 characters. A verification link is mailed and stays valid for 24 hours; until the address is confirmed, signing in is refused with `email_not_verified`.

Registration creates your first organization and makes you its Owner. Each organization has its own customers, invoices, notifications and audit trail, and your role can differ from one organization to another.

Sign in with the same address and password. The dashboard shows outstanding and overdue totals, invoice counts by status, and paid revenue for the last twelve months.

The sidebar leads to the dashboard, customers, invoices, the team, notifications, the audit trail and settings. A Viewer sees the same read-only pages; a write the role does not allow is answered as not found, so a permission boundary is not disclosed.

Signing out ends the session on the server. Changing your password signs out every other browser.
